## Portable creative formula

> A **savvy insider** is **silently reacting to something on their own screen** when **a high-stakes claim about their own profession lands as on-screen text** ("this just replaced my entire X"). Camera **stays in intimate close-up, then hard-cuts to a tactile screen-recording POV where a physical prop guides the viewer's eye through a 3-step demo against a recognised third-party brand**. They **reveal an end-state in compressed time** (a wall of finished, on-brand output), which sets up **the feeling that the viewer has just been let in on an unfair advantage**. Closes on **the finished artefact filling frame plus a comment-keyword CTA to unlock access**.

## 5–7 non-negotiables (must survive any rebuild)

1. **Silent-shock cold open.** Face-only close-up, no VO, on-screen text carries the high-stakes claim. Expression does the work — over-acting kills it.
2. **Occupational-threat hook.** The claim is "X just replaced [thing the viewer pays for / does for a living]." It must read as a real professional anxiety, not a gimmick.
3. **Physical-to-digital bridge.** A real-world prop (pen, finger, marker) points at a real screen at least once before any pure screen recording. Grounds the AI in tactile reality.
4. **Third-party recognised brand as the input.** The demo runs against a brand the viewer already trusts (Lululemon here). The tool's intelligence is validated by making *someone else's* brand look right, not by self-praise.
5. **3-step UI compression.** Copy → paste → generate. No more than three visible actions before the payoff. Anything longer and the "magic" dies.
6. **Time-compressed reveal of finished output at scale.** The payoff is *volume × on-brand-ness in seconds*, not a single polished asset. The grid matters.
7. **Comment-keyword CTA.** Engagement-bait unlock as the closer. Algorithm-friendly, low-friction, leaves dopamine debt.

## Swap variables (change per brand)

- **Character archetype** — savvy insider here, but could be exhausted founder, junior employee, sceptical exec. Must be plausibly the person threatened by the tool.
- **Occupational threat in the hook** — "marketing team" is the lever. Could be "video editor", "designer", "SDR", "lawyer" depending on tool category.
- **Recognised third-party brand used as demo input** — Lululemon was high-trust premium retail. Pick a brand your audience instantly judges output against; not a competitor.
- **Tool being demonstrated** — the actual product slot.
- **Demonstrated payoff artefact** — grid of ads here. Could be a deck, a video edit, a contract, a sales sequence. Must be visually dense at the reveal.
- **CTA keyword** — "VIBE" here. Brand-aligned, easy to spell, unique enough to track.
- **Prop choice** — pink pen is on-trend; pick something character-consistent.

## Transferability flags

- **Beat-by-beat camera detail returned by Gemini is incomplete** (only 2 of ~7 beats covered). Will need to be re-derived per shot at concept stage — don't trust the camera array as-is.
- **The pacing depends on cut-on-text-change.** If the rebuild's tool flow has more than 3 UI states, the formula breaks. Compress or skip steps; don't extend.
- **Audio is load-bearing.** No-VO works *because* the music does the emotional pacing. Swap to a track at similar BPM (≈125) and energy curve (spike → bed → spike).
